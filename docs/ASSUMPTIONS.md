# Assumptions

Choices the data forced, with the reason for each. Each one names where it is
applied. Change one only with a reason recorded here.

## Phase 1: atlas

| Assumption                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Why                                                                                                                                                             | Where                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| The EDOM peak hour counts trips by arrival time, in one-hour windows stepped every 15 minutes; "motorised" means main-mode codes 1 to 14.                                                                                                                                                         | The report does not state its method. This rule reproduces its figures (220,815 and 164,368) to within 0.02%; departure, midpoint and in-progress rules do not. | `pipeline/src/sim_poa/atlas/edom.py`, `peak_by_arrival` |
| Departure profiles use a sliding one-hour window, stepped every 15 minutes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Respondents round times to the hour and half hour, which turns 15-minute bins into alternating peaks and gaps.                                                  | `edom.py`, `departure_profile`                          |
| Desire lines include only zone pairs with at least 5 survey records.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Pairs with fewer records are dominated by expansion noise and come closest to identifying individual households.                                                | `edom.py`, `MIN_RECORDS_PER_PAIR`                       |
| A count interval without a value is missing, not zero; an hour counts only when all of it was counted.                                                                                                                                                                                            | Incomplete surveys stop part-way through the day; treating gaps as zero would invent low traffic.                                                               | `counts_build.py`, `hourly_totals`                      |
| A count movement without a number in the header gets its position in the sheet.                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Several workbooks leave some `FLUXO` numbers blank; the position matches the numbering used in the filled ones.                                                 | `counts.py`, `find_blocks`                              |
| A two-day survey date ("19 e 20/08/2026") is dated by its first day.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Only one date is stored per survey.                                                                                                                             | `counts.py`, `parse_date`                               |
| A survey year before 1990 or after the current year is a typing error. If a folder in the path is a year containing the typed digits in order (208 in `2008`), that year is used and the survey is flagged `ano-corrigido-pela-pasta`; otherwise the date is dropped and flagged `data-invalida`. | Three sheets say 207 or 208. Only one sits in a year folder; for the other two, 2007 and 2017 are equally possible.                                             | `counts.py`, `plausible_date`                           |
| A site name "X entre A e B" is placed at the crossing of X with A ("trecho").                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | It marks the block, not a point; the first crossing is a fixed, reproducible choice. Flagged as approximate on the map.                                         | `geocode.py`                                            |
| Two streets that do not touch within 50 m are not located. Within 50 m, the midpoint of their closest points is used ("proximidade").                                                                                                                                                             | Squares are drawn as outlines, tens of metres from the street axis.                                                                                             | `geocode.py`, `NEAR_METRES`                             |
| An address outside every numbering range is placed at the nearest numbered stretch if within 300 numbers ("endereço-aproximado").                                                                                                                                                                 | The centreline numbering has gaps, and on dual carriageways one side is often zero.                                                                             | `geocode.py`, `ADDRESS_SLACK`                           |
| Speeds below 3 km/h or above 200 km/h are dropped.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | They are typing or radar errors, not measurements.                                                                                                              | `speeds.py`, `PLAUSIBLE_KMH`                            |
| A speed survey's direction comes from the sheet when present, otherwise from the file name.                                                                                                                                                                                                       | The two disagree in some files; the sheet is the field record.                                                                                                  | `speeds.py`                                             |
| Bus GPS uses the October 2019 week only, weekdays.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | The July 2021 week falls in the pandemic period the guide suggests excluding.                                                                                   | `bus_gps.py`                                            |
| Bus GPS speed is the median of moving buses (above 3 km/h), with the share stopped shown separately; segment-hours need at least 30 points.                                                                                                                                                       | Dwell at stops would otherwise pull the median towards zero on every segment with a stop.                                                                       | `bus_gps.py`                                            |
| Buses per stop use a Wednesday in the middle of the GTFS validity.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | A typical weekday, away from the start and end of the feed.                                                                                                     | `transit.py`, `typical_weekday`                         |

## Phase 2: road network

| Assumption                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Where                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| OSM classes map onto the four urban classes of the traffic code (art. 60) as: `motorway`, `trunk` and their links are trânsito rápido; `primary`, `secondary` and their links are arterial; `tertiary` and its link are coletora; `unclassified`, `residential` and `living_street` are local. Where a road has no `maxspeed`, it gets the art. 61 default for its class (80, 60, 40 or 30 km/h). | The code defines the classes by function, not by OSM tag, and no city classification has been found yet. The mapping is this project's judgement.                                                                                                                                         | `pipeline/src/sim_poa/network/speeds.py` |
| The municipal boundary is the union of the unsimplified EDOM zones whose `CD_MUN` is Porto Alegre.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | The simplified zones used by the atlas leave gaps between neighbours, and their union drew false boundaries inside the city (Av. Ipiranga came out as a city entry).                                                                                                                      | `clip.py`, `city_outline`                |
| The network keeps every OSM road class from `living_street` up, and drops `service`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Service roads are car parks and driveways; they add tens of thousands of edges without carrying through traffic.                                                                                                                                                                          | `clip.py`, `ROAD_CLASSES`                |
| Signals outside junctions are kept (no `--tls.discard-simple`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | 797 of the 1,555 inventory records are type `EXCLUSIVO`: 790 of them have no vehicle crossing (`CRUZ_VE` = 0) and 517 say "PEDESTRE" in the description, so they are mid-block pedestrian signals. They stop cars like any other signal. SUMO gives each one green, yellow and a 5 s red. | `convert.py`, `NETCONVERT_OPTIONS`       |
| A network signal and an inventory record match when they are within 30 m. A missing inventory signal is switched on at the nearest network junction within 30 m. A network signal inside the city is switched off only when no inventory record lies within 80 m. Outside the city nothing changes.                                                                                               | 30 m covers a large junction; the wider 80 m for switching off avoids removing OSM signals tagged a few metres before the junction they belong to. The inventory covers only Porto Alegre.                                                                                                | `checks.py`, `signal_corrections`        |
| Zone connectivity routes between one anchor edge per zone: the drivable edge nearest the zone's representative point among the edges inside it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Ten large southern zones have their representative point more than 300 m from any road, though each contains over 100 drivable edges. Demand in phase 3 uses all edges of a zone.                                                                                                         | `checks.py`, `zone_anchors`              |
| A corridor runs between the two edges with its name that lie furthest apart inside the city. The route follows the avenue: other roads count 20 times their travel time.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | The fastest free-flow route between the ends often leaves the avenue (Assis Brasil via the Freeway, for example), which would not time the corridor.                                                                                                                                      | `checks.py`, `corridor_free_flow`        |
| Corridor names follow OSM: "Avenida Farrapos", and "Avenida Doutor Nilo Peçanha" for Nilo Peçanha.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | These are the names the network carries. OSM names only about 1.7 km Edvaldo Pereira Paiva, so that corridor is short; whether the rest of the riverside avenue has another name in OSM is not checked yet.                                                                               | `checks.py`, `CORRIDORS`                 |
